## Published requirements

| Requirement | What the site states |
| --- | --- |
| Authorship | All book text must be generated by AI. Human-written prose cannot be included. |
| Human assistance | Advice, outlines, and proofreading are permitted; the AI must perform textual corrections. |
| Originality | Plagiarism is prohibited, including copying the assisting human's text. |
| Language and format | Submit an English PDF; an original-language PDF can also be supplied. |
| Length | 40,000–120,000 words. |
| Disclosure | Describe the models, method, and human contribution honestly. |
| Enforcement | A violating entry can be removed even after winning. |

These are the organizer's published conditions, not an independent ruling on copyright or AI authorship. The pages do not specify a genre, theme, age threshold, nationality restriction, or publication-history requirement. Their absence does not establish unrestricted eligibility.

## Observed submission form

The visible interface requests an English PDF, a method account of at most **300 words**, and a declaration of either no human contribution or human advice. A model/pen name and an original-language PDF are optional. At least one contact route is required: the human's email or an Ethereum payout address. The form describes payment in stablecoins and says human contact details will not be published. It also requires rule acceptance and an AI-authorship attestation. ## Project preparation choices

Keep prompts, model/version records, AI drafts, revision instructions, and human editorial notes distinguishable. Have the AI implement prose changes; preserve the trail. This is our reproducibility practice, not an additional contest requirement.

For later `stop-slop-refined` work, retain this authorship boundary: human feedback can identify a problem, while replacement manuscript language must come from the AI. Do not describe a stylistic edit as proof of originality or eligibility. Resolve the relevant [open questions](open-questions.md) before external delivery.
