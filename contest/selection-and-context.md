# Selection and context

Checked **2026-10-03**. Claims below are separated from corroborated context and project interpretation.

## What the organizer proposes

The [home page](https://thelyson.ai/#accueil) describes Seer markets selecting approximately ten finalists; the highest-ranked entry receives a **$5,000 Grand Prix**, increased by patron contributions. Human jurors read finalists and award a separate **$1,000 Special Mention**. Its FAQ says the markets anticipate juror opinion and verified humans receive participation credits. Exact scoring and settlement rules are not supplied.

The [jury form](https://thelyson.ai/#jury) requires applicants to commit to complete reading, abstain from the markets, keep rankings secret until results, and accept publication of their application and juror status. No named final jury or dated ceremony schedule was visible.

## Funding and organizer evidence

The visible [patron wall](https://thelyson.ai/#mecenes) displayed **Seer One — $5,000**. The site's [wall JSON](https://thelyson.ai/wall.json) also contains that entry, but its own timestamp is **2026-09-29**, so this is a dated site snapshot. Page code treats Seer One's base as a commitment when calculating the headline prize.

We have not reconciled its balance, treasury, transfers, payout authority, or restrictions. Neither the displayed amount nor a contract link proves escrow or a guaranteed award payment.

The site's contact is `prixnouvellesecritures@gmail.com`. No named individual or legal organizing entity appeared in the inspected pages. Sponsorship does not establish who operates the competition. [Prize site](https://thelyson.ai/)

## What Seer documentation establishes

[Seer's official site](https://seer.pm/) links its [documentation](https://seer-3.gitbook.io/seer-documentation), which describes several market types, outcome tokens, Reality.eth resolution, and Kleros disputes. Those capabilities explain the platform generally. They do not establish this contest's market addresses, oracle questions, ranking metric, trading window, or dispute policy. No contest market was verified in this research.

## Why the name matters

The prize expressly describes its name as satirical and disclaims Thélyson Orélien's endorsement. An invitation displayed on the site does not establish his acceptance. [Prize site](https://thelyson.ai/)

The [Académie Goncourt's statement dated 25 September 2026](https://www.academiegoncourt.com/home) confirms that it removed *C'était ça ou mourir* from its initial selection, citing plagiarism revelations and analyses suggesting substantial AI generation. Those are the Academy's stated reasons, not an independent determination of how the novel was written. The prize's rules link the [Academy's statement PDF](https://www.academiegoncourt.com/_files/ugd/d5bd15_0d57d8368a57400da14f59ebc4021bed.pdf); the text was verified on the Academy's HTML page.

**Project interpretation:** the competition responds satirically to that controversy while proposing a real award. This does not imply affiliation with the author or the Academy. Our manuscript can be developed on its own literary merits; the site specifies no requirement to write about AI or the controversy.
