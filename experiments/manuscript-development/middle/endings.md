# Two endings to test

4 October 2026. Author proposals following the actual warehouse and boat-shed candidates. Neither is a human preference result or a completed whole-book payoff. Both preserve one 07:00-to-07:00 return and an irreversible departure. Exact evening invitation, intervening choices and river conditions need enacted setup.

## A — Stay at the birthday gathering

Flora receives an explicit dinner invitation and remains as Jo's friends leave. She and Jo wash up, quarrel over something mundane they have postponed, then attempt a shared activity that does not fit the old mother-child division: finishing a small repair beneath the new table or making breakfast dough for after Flora has gone. Leo returns early in the morning. Flora chooses another half-hour with Jo over the proposed boat outing; she tells Leo herself, without making Jo carry the request. They meet the car together outside the flat before 07:00. Jo keeps the usable, imperfect table and a morning she must finish without Flora.

Necessary setup: Jo must actively want some late company, rather than merely permit it; Leo's wish to row must have real weight, including his disappointment; the final activity must matter beyond a symbolic memorial. Flora cannot receive a perfect account of all fifteen years or make her departure into everybody else's examination.

Cost: the bodily desire established at the boat shed goes unfulfilled. That can be powerful if chosen, but it risks making the whole novel return to the original aim of unlimited proximity to Jo and reducing Leo's work to a lesson in accommodation. The ending needs to retain the lost outing rather than presenting the decision as unquestionably right.

## B — Leave the gathering, walk, then row at dawn

After an actual dinner invitation and a genuinely good portion of the evening, Flora chooses to leave with Leo. The reason includes her own wish to walk with him and try the boat, alongside fatigue and uncertainty about how to stay at a gathering which now has its own momentum. Jo is disappointed or annoyed in a way Flora can observe; her response cannot be narrated as secret relief. Flora says when and where they can meet before collection. Jo does not need to promise attendance to make the choice possible.

Flora and Leo visit his present cottage and walk to the river in the early morning. They launch only if conditions permit, with suitable flotation, a short route and a landing time comfortably before 07:00. Flora rows imperfectly. Leo's familiar company and unfamiliar skill coexist; a small shared success need not settle their argument about the old house. They come ashore, stow the boat and find Jo already there or see her arrive while there is still time for an ordinary exchange. Jo's presence is her chosen action, not a summoned reward. The car collects Flora from a reachable place at 07:00. End with a particular physical act or sensation from Flora's perspective, not a posthumous view of the survivors or an explanation of what the day meant.

Necessary setup: Flora's appetite for walking and rowing must already be shown; Leo needs a present life worth entering, not merely a picturesque vehicle for goodbye; Jo and Flora must accomplish something at the table before Flora leaves. The place and time of the final meeting must be stated while Jo can hear them. Do not conceal plans merely to produce a surprise reunion. If bad weather prevents launching, an equally concrete shared task is a credible fallback, not a supernatural weather concession.

Cost: leaving after having pressed for access may hurt Jo. Dawn rowing can look suspiciously tidy if all three people appear content with the schedule. Retain one unresolved disappointment, an imperfect practical detail and the fact that the next day belongs to Jo and Leo without Flora.

## Working recommendation

Develop **B**, because the middle test supplies Flora with a second desired experience that comes from her own body and curiosity. It also permits Jo to act in the final hour without being kept awake as an audience all night. Preserve A as a structural alternative if the whole draft fails to make the evening departure credible. This recommendation selects a direction for drafting, not a claim that B is intrinsically better or approved by the human reader.
