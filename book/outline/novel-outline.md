## Working choices

Merewick is an invented English Midlands river town. Saturday 16 May 2026, 07:00, to Sunday 17 May, 07:00. Distances, buildings, businesses and local navigation arrangements are invented; no claim that a real town has these facilities. Ordinary bodies tire, eat, sleep and feel pain. Flora remembers her life before her death, not the intervening years or an explained afterlife. Return was arranged while she was ill; no new terminal-disease plot or last-minute exemption. The service collects her just before seven; the legal and physical machinery remains outside her knowledge. End the book at the established boundary, without extending it.

Flora died at 41, Leo was 44 and is now 59, Jo was 15 and is 30 today. Flora wants to make a new shared experience with each of them, but she also wants to remain the person who best knows them. She is often funny and often wrong, not a pupil completing a day of ethical lessons. Jo wants a good dinner in a home she has made functional, with friends who know her adult self; her work is building-conservation surveying/technician work, not qualified structural engineering. Leo wants to share a skill acquired after Flora's death. He has sold the former family house, lives alone in a small cottage, and had a substantial later relationship with Catherine that ended two years ago. It was real and sometimes happy; no villain or secret widow competition. Bess is his older rowing friend, not a partner.

Jo rents a first-floor flat near Harper Street. Its entrance is 74 cm clear with a turn; the route must be shown to have room for the detached 118 cm table top upright. The table has removable legs, a small old mark and later one new edge chip made by Flora while carrying. Jo buys it for use, not as a symbolic inheritance. No active woodworm confirmed. Jo's planned six diners include Jo, Arun, Nina, Mara, Phil and her aunt Ellen (Flora's older sister). Patrick, Nina's partner, is a possible seventh. Flora's later invitation brings the actual total to eight when Patrick comes; use the old folding table as an additional surface rather than fitting eight comfortably around 118 cm. Leo elects not to stay for dinner; nobody must expel him. Arun is Jo's friend and works in a restaurant; romance is not implied.
