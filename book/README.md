## Working files

- `brief/`: concept, audience, narrative choices, and voice notes.
- `outline/`: scene sequence and structural revisions once the concept is selected.
- `chapters/`: manuscript only, named `001-short-title.md`, `002-short-title.md`, and so on. Keep research, prompts, reviewer notes, and alternate versions outside the final chapter sequence.
- `continuity/`: the [story bible](continuity/story-bible.md), timeline, character knowledge, and open narrative promises.

Use Markdown as the editable manuscript source; choose the PDF export route after the book's needs are clear. Chapter order must be explicit when packaging. A word-count check will count the assembled manuscript, excluding project docs and research.

Every drafting or substantive revision session needs a [generation record](../provenance/README.md). Feedback from the human is welcome; all final wording must be generated by AI.
