# The book

*The Fifteenth Year* is a complete, revised English novel of **22 chapters and 44,552 prose words**, as recorded in the [final manifest](reviews/final-manifest.json) on 4 October 2026. The count excludes chapter titles and scene separators. It is below the initial 45,000–50,000-word estimate and within the published 40,000–120,000-word range; no material was added merely to reach the estimate.

Begin with [Seven](chapters/01-seven.md). The [chapter manifest](manifest.json) defines reading and assembly order. The [creative brief](brief/creative-brief.md), [outline](outline/novel-outline.md) and [story bible](continuity/story-bible.md) explain the adopted direction and continuity.

## What has been completed

The user selected the concept on 4 October after reading the German opening, then authorized autonomous execution. The AI developed the warehouse and boat-shed scenes, compared endings, chose the setting and wrote the complete English manuscript. The two earlier English sources were deliberately imported into chapter 1; their [original pins](brief/source-baseline.json) remain unchanged.

The first draft contains 44,851 prose words and is preserved at commit `38e7942b5e4e6fe62baec71101f860efac34eeef`. The [whole-book review](reviews/2026-10-04-whole-book-review.md) records structural decisions. The [revision record](reviews/revision-01-changes.json) identifies every applied edit, including the repeated afternoon scene mechanism and the contradictory recollection across chapters 14–15. Separate agents verified the resulting [early/middle passages](reviews/2026-10-04-revision-01-a-verification.md) and [late passages](reviews/2026-10-04-revision-01-c-verification.md). Those are bounded AI reviews, not human reader approval or a prize assessment.

## Working files

- `brief/`: selected premise, current choices and preserved source baseline.
- `outline/`: the complete chapter sequence and adopted setting.
- `chapters/`: English manuscript only, named `01-seven.md`, `02-the-underside.md`, and so on. Alternate candidates and editorial records remain outside this sequence.
- `continuity/`: current story bible and historical batch handoffs.
- `reviews/`: source manifests, first-draft findings, applied changes and verification evidence.
- `reading/de/`: text-only German excerpts, including the [café](reading/de/02-das-cafe.md) and [warehouse](reading/de/03-die-unterseite.md). These are reading translations, not a complete German novel or part of the English entry count.
