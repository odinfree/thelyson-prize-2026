## One additional ambiguity to simplify

**C06, low/clarification:** [chapter18](../chapters/18-a-message.md), line61, says the last visible message in Leo's chat with Jo gives the time he would meet Flora. Chapter15 presents the22:00 arrangement as news to Jo. The text does not date the message, so a later message could make both true; this is **not a proven contradiction**. Its unnecessary precision invites the wrong inference. Remove the meeting-time detail or replace it with an established ordinary household topic. Preserve Flora's choice not to read the rest of the chat.

## Late-sequence structure

- **Departure is a real choice.** Chapter15 lets Jo say that Flora could stay now she knows Jo wants her; Flora says she wants to go with Leo. Chapter16 then makes the road back available and has Flora decline it. This protects agency against the interpretation that she was expelled or left solely to avoid accountability. Chapter11's fixed22:00 walk and chapter9's physical interest supply earlier setup; those context scenes are not independent-review results here.
- **Jo has agency without rewarding the mother on demand.** Chapter15 leaves attendance undecided; chapter18's call leaves it possible and allows Jo to want sleep; chapter20 shows her physically arriving and negotiating the gate. Chapter21 has her help with the boat, raise her own housing disagreement, bring food and ask for a photograph. Neither her arrival nor her affection claims the evening hurt has vanished.
- **Leo has a present life and a skill.** His chosen supper and unfinished film, limited recollection of Jo's request, and refusal to invent a perfect memory keep him from being only transport. Chapter17 gives Catherine ordinary competence without a replacement-wife contest. Chapter20 has him teach and sometimes stop Flora. These are observable scene functions, not reader-preference scores.
- **The rowing is earned on the page.** Chapter19 lets Flora hesitate before leaving the cottage yet choose to go; preparation and waiting for daylight have physical substance. Chapter20 includes poor strokes, short success, fatigue and a voluntary turn back before a crisis. No accident, magical extension or wisdom delivered by the current supplies the payoff.
- **The last three chapters do different work.**20 is the new experience;21 returns them to shared labour and an unresolved father/daughter disagreement;22 settles possessions and enacts collection. A separate Jo/Leo goodbye is proportionate because the relationships differ. Chapter22 ends as the dashboard changes to07:00, with no assertion of extra lived time or omniscient survivors' future.

## Repetition and optional compression

There are several emotionally complete pauses before the final car: the flat hug, bridge tenderness, photographs/bed, midnight call, cottage threshold, landing arrival and bank silence. They currently change the practical relationship or decision, so I do not recommend removing a whole scene solely because it resembles a farewell. The best tightening opportunity is **commentary which explains the action twice**, especially chapter16's second account of why the concert story pleased Flora, and sentences in19–20 explicitly resisting turning ordinary details or rowing into lessons. These are optional literary judgments; a contextual line pass should test removal against nearby voice and timing, not apply a ban on reflection or repetition.

The housing dispute is intentionally not resolved. Retain it. The imperfect photo and final dirty fingernail keep Jo particular rather than making her an emblem; neither needs a following explanatory moral.
