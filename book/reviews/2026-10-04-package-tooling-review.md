## Tool behavior and addressed limitation

The assembler fails on unordered/nonconsecutive chapter numbering, duplicate or undeclared chapter files, missing required chapters, escaping source paths, empty prose and unexpected internal headings. It retains original chapter bytes in the fingerprint rather than hashing a normalized rendering. The export refuses an incomplete or out-of-range declared manuscript.

The exporter escapes literal XML-sensitive characters before passing prose to ReportLab, preserving source punctuation rather than interpreting manuscript text as arbitrary markup. It joins internal paragraph line breaks and renders the explicit scene separator. It does not silently strip inline Markdown. The complete token/character comparisons demonstrate that this actual manuscript has not lost prose through that conversion. Keeping the final small block of each chapter together changes pagination, not the compared source sequence.

An initially inspected verifier compared only non-whitespace characters. That could miss a joined or split word even though it caught deleted or reordered characters. The integrator strengthened it during review: the pinned current version also requires `expected.split() == actual.split()` in its pass condition. The current PDF passes the stronger check independently. No further refactor is needed to resolve this issue for the delivered artifact.

The verifier compares against current declared sources and records their fingerprint. It does not itself load and enforce the separate frozen final manifest or build sidecar. For this delivery the independent cross-check above explicitly confirms all four agree. Preserve this step after any source or PDF replacement rather than assuming a stale report remains applicable.

## Limits retained

Text extraction cannot establish detailed visual quality, paragraph spacing, page-number appearance, or how the pages feel to read. The integrator's package report separately records all 167 pages inspected through contact sheets and full-resolution samples on pages 2, 65, 104, 112, 149 and 167. This reviewer read that record but does not claim to have repeated its visual inspection.

The build sidecar's `checks_remaining` list describes the export-stage state. The later extraction and package records supply the subsequent evidence; a successful build alone would not have done so. Dates in PDF metadata are generated export metadata, not provenance proof. No originality, authorship, literary quality, rules interpretation, submission or organizer acceptance is established by these mechanical checks.

**Disposition:** the current exact PDF is mechanically consistent with the final source package. Keep it unsent pending the separate submission instruction and retain the hashes with the package record.
