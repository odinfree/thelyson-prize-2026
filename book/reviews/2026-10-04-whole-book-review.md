## Structural decision

Keep the22-chapter day and irreversible ending. The table purchase and damage, Jo's professional mistake and dinner, Leo's house sale and later relationship, the shared oar repair, Flora's public storytelling error, chosen walk and short dawn outing form a complete sequence. Each major relationship obtains action beyond explaining the premise. Leo's evening at home and Jo's uncertain dawn attendance matter because Flora cannot secure every desired hour. No new danger, late revelation, magical exception or additional chapter is needed to make the ending happen.

The main structural weakness is repeated displacement in the afternoon: Catherine, Jo's previous boat outings and Ellen's private conversations all threaten Flora's special knowledge. Chapter9 now retains the necessary Jo-trip information but turns promptly towards curiosity and useful work. It no longer supplies another extended accusation-and-reassurance exchange. The actual repair, dry rowing lesson and bodily desire survive; these are needed for the later departure choice.

The chapter14/15 recollection conflict changes the meaning of Flora's apology. The chosen repair preserves uncertain recollection in15 and16: she believes Jo about a promise she cannot recover. Chapter14 retains her remembered pre-concert anxiety and actual public correction of the distorted story, but loses the incompatible recovered promise. Public repair does not cancel the private injury or secure permission to leave.

## Prose decisions

Keep the dry humor, repeated domestic objects and narrator's mixed motives. Their repetition is not automatically a defect. Preserve the mild story told to Arun as genuine social pleasure and setup for the later confidence breach. Keep Catherine's place among six deliberately chosen pictures, Ellen's uneven earrings, Jo's altered recipe and the unsolved Jo/Leo rental disagreement.

Cut a small set of explanatory clauses after actions already make the emotion apparent: the cable-filled cake tin, stopped library recommendation, table chip and conservation discussion. Late chapters also lose clauses that explicitly refuse to make sticks, pan washing or rowing into lessons. These are bounded editorial choices, not a lexical blacklist. The book retains reflection when it reveals something not already supplied by the action.
