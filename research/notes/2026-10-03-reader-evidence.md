# Reader evidence and bounded revision

As of 3 October 2026, four original opening directions remain unselected. There are no human reader results, complete manuscript or official contest scores. The private research campaign has expanded from diagnosis to a small actual prose repair and to preparation for reader comparisons.

## What to measure

Our proposed reader pass asks what someone wants to continue, where understanding fails and which choices matter to them. It keeps those responses separate from stylistic diagnostics. An expressed wish to read more remains an intention unless further reading actually occurs. Four different openings compare four particular executions; they do not isolate genre, a writing algorithm or one editing technique.

The additional source register contains [suspense research](https://doi.org/10.1017/langcog.2025.10057), [a spoiler experiment](https://ssol-journal.com/articles/190/files/6818ab3f892e8.pdf), [eye-tracking work](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04043), [individual differences in liking](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41235-022-00419-0), [twist rereading](https://benjamins.com/catalog/ssol.17002.kom) and [local information-gap experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2701237/). Primary methods were checked with access limits recorded. These are short-text studies addressing different outcomes; their results do not supply a single novel-writing objective.

Our practical inference is to collect free reactions before targeted diagnostics, retain reading order and partial exposure, permit ties and uncertainty, and show individual responses. A proposed small panel can guide an editorial decision. Its answers should not be pooled into a supposedly objective literary score, and repeated responses from one reader are not independent readers. No recruitment has occurred.

## A repair the workflow can inspect

One private opening had a fictional time limit in its planning brief that was not fully available in the prose. An independent AI specification identified two repair targets and seven preservation constraints before one new candidate was saved. The candidate changed one exchange and retained every outside byte. A separate AI review judged both targets achieved and all constraints preserved, while leaving aesthetic preference unassessed and interpretive uncertainty visible.

An offline replay then preserved old/new source identities, marked affected text records stale, and associated the review with the exact original, brief and candidate. Identical text under a different revision could not silently inherit that assessment. This is a local engineering and task-completion observation, not a reader preference finding, a blinded comparison or a public benchmark. The private raw materials are not reproduced here.

## Consequences for later Stop Slop work

The transfer candidate is a more precise editing workflow: name the intended repair, preserve constraints, keep source references stable and assess the resulting prose separately from the diagnosis. Positive action labels and exact quotations are insufficient by themselves. The two tested prompt overlays have not earned adoption; no upstream code or default behavior has changed. Reader preference and successful local repair remain separate evidence requirements.
